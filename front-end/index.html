About this demo


    

  • Abstract Factory: the country you pick determines whether a LATAMPaymentFactory, EUPaymentFactory or USPaymentFactory is instantiated on the server.
    • 

  • Bridge: the Payment Type you pick determines whether a SubscriptionPayment or OneTimePayment abstraction is used, independently of the underlying gateway.
    • 

  • Adapter: the factory injects a MercadoPagoAdapter, StripeAdapter or LocalBankISOAdapter as the concrete PaymentGateway implementor, translating standard calls into each provider's own API.
